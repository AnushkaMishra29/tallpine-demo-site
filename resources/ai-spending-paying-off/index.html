Is our AI spending paying off?

Most leadership teams can list what they spent on AI. Far fewer can show what came back.

Spending is the easy half to count: licences, cloud bills and the hours your people put in. The other half is harder, because the benefit turns up inside other teams' work and nobody is asked to write it down.

A useful answer starts with one page per project. It says who is responsible, what the starting point was, what the project was meant to change, and what has been measured since.

With that in place, the update to your board becomes a short list: projects that have delivered, projects that are still a forecast, and projects you stopped, with the reason.

In short

  • Give every project a named owner and a starting point
  • Keep forecasts and measured results apart
  • Report stopped projects too, with the reason
