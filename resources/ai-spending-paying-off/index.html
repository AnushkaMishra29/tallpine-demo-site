Board question

Is our AI spending paying off?

Most leadership teams can list what they spent on AI. Far fewer can show what came back.

Spending is easy to count: licences, cloud bills, the team's time. Returns are harder, because they show up inside other people's work and nobody is asked to claim them.

Tallpine keeps one record per initiative, from the first idea to the result Finance has reviewed. Each one carries an owner, a baseline, a target and the evidence behind the current figure.

The answer to the board is then a list, not a slide: which initiatives have delivered, which are still a forecast, and which were stopped and why.
