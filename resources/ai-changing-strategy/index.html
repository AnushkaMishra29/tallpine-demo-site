How is AI changing strategy?

When almost anything can be built in a week, deciding what not to build becomes the real work.

Teams now propose more AI projects in a quarter than they used to in a year. Without a way to compare them, the loudest sponsor wins.

Tallpine ties every proposal to a focus area the leadership team has already agreed, and asks the same few questions of each one before it gets a budget.

Strategy stops being a yearly document and becomes a weekly habit: a short review, a clear yes or no, and a written reason either way.
