How is AI changing strategy?

When almost anything can be built in a week, deciding what not to build becomes the real work.

Teams now pitch more AI projects in a quarter than they used to in a year. Without a common way to compare them, the best-argued pitch wins, which is not always the most useful one.

The fix is simple and takes discipline: agree a handful of priority themes for the year, then ask every proposal the same few questions before it gets time or money.

Strategy then stops being a yearly document. It becomes a short weekly review with a clear yes or no and a written reason either way.

In short

  • Agree a few priority themes for the year
  • Ask every proposal the same questions
  • Write down each yes and each no
