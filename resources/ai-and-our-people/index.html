What does AI mean for our people?

A tool that nobody uses changes nothing. Adoption has to be planned like any other part of the work.

Every initiative in Tallpine names the group of people whose work it is meant to change, and how many of them need to be using it for the plan to hold.

Usage is read from the systems people already work in, so nobody fills in a survey and nobody guesses.

When adoption stalls, the owner sees it in the same place as the budget and the timeline, early enough to do something about it.
