About this site

Tallpine is a made-up company, built to demonstrate how an inbound engine handles website enquiries.

This site is part of a portfolio project in go-to-market engineering. Nothing here is a real product, customer or offer, and the company does not exist.

The site has two jobs. First, its forms send what you type to a test CRM, where an inbound engine works out who is writing, chooses a route, drafts a reply from approved wording and sets a one-business-day deadline. A person approves and sends every reply; nothing is sent automatically.

Second, it gives a visitor-identification script (Swan) real pages to watch. That script estimates which company a visit comes from, and it can sometimes match a visit to a person. The engine keeps only the company, discards anything about a person, and never sends a message because of a visit alone.

If you try the forms, please use made-up details.
