Privacy

What this demonstration site collects, in plain words.

Forms

What you type into a form is sent to a HubSpot test account used only for this demonstration. Please enter made-up details. No phone number is asked for or stored.

Visitor identification

The site may load a script from Swan that estimates which company a visit comes from, based on the network the visit arrives through. It is an estimate about an organization. The project keeps only the company name and the pages visited; any detail about an individual is discarded before anything is stored or scored.

No marketing

Nobody is added to a mailing list, and no message is ever sent because of a visit.
